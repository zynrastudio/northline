# Redesign Opportunities (Before → After)

This document captures intentional limitations of the **before** Northline site. They are product/strategy gaps, not implementation bugs. Use them as the brief for the future redesign case study.

Source: [prd.md](./prd.md) — Future Opportunities.

## Positioning & messaging

- Broad “full-service” claim with little differentiation
- Tagline-level creativity messaging (“Creative Solutions for Modern Businesses”) without a sharp niche
- Limited outcome, process, or specialization storytelling

## Audience & structure

- No audience segmentation (SMB, startup, ecommerce, etc.)
- Information architecture mirrors internal services, not the buyer journey
- No industry-specific landing pages

## Services & conversion

- Services presented as equal offerings, not outcome packages
- Minimal process, deliverables, and ideal-client detail
- Single general contact form as the “consultation request”
- No qualification questions, discovery funnel, or scheduling

## Case studies & content

- Portfolio is visual/descriptive (overview, services, gallery)
- Little challenge / thinking / process / measurable impact
- Blog is tip/update oriented rather than strategic proof

## Systems & operations

- Email-only lead capture (no CRM)
- No automation, lead scoring, or marketing workflows
- Basic analytics only (pageviews + form conversion)

## Visual system

- Competent corporate brochure look: blue accents, rounded cards, stock photography, minimal motion
- Generic enough that a redesign can show a clear before/after contrast

## Out of scope for before (do not backfill here)

Client portal, CRM workflows, calculators, personalization, proposal generation, advanced analytics.

---

When starting the **after** site: create branch `after`, Vercel project `northline-after`, and keep `before` / `northline-before` unchanged except for critical fixes.
