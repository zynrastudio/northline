# Project Overview

Northline Creative is a creative and digital agency that helps businesses improve their online presence through websites, branding, marketing, design, and digital strategy.

The company has grown primarily through referrals and repeat clients, and now requires a modern website to showcase its capabilities.

This PRD intentionally defines the business requirements for a website that is functional but strategically underdeveloped. The objective is to establish a believable "before" experience that can later be redesigned into a significantly stronger product.

This document focuses on Northline's actual business requirements rather than creating problems solely for the redesign narrative. The shortcomings should emerge naturally from reasonable decisions commonly found in many growing agencies.

# Summary

Northline Creative's website successfully establishes an online presence and communicates the breadth of its services.

The experience is professional, functional, and visually competent, allowing visitors to understand what the agency offers and how to make contact.

However, the website reflects the characteristics of a broad creative agency serving many audiences with many services. While suitable for an agency growing through referrals, it does little to communicate a distinctive market position or guide prospective clients toward a focused engagement.

This foundation provides a realistic starting point for a future redesign that emphasizes specialization, clearer messaging, stronger conversion strategy, and a more intentional customer journey.
